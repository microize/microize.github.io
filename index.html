Hi, I'm Sripathi M.

Data & AI Architect. I build the layer that tells enterprise AI what the data means.

Ten years in engineering, seven in data, the last four at Fractal - enterprise data architecture, governance and data modelling on Azure and Databricks, across supply chain, consumer goods and retail. Today I architect the logistics ontology and the semantic layer beneath it for a Fortune 500 CPG company.

Ask three teams in a large company for their on-time delivery figure and you get three different answers - each correct by its own definition. People resolve that ambiguity in a meeting. An AI agent cannot: given inconsistent definitions it returns a confident answer that is simply wrong.

Most of my decade has gone into the layers underneath that problem - ETL and dimensional modelling, then lakehouse platforms, then the architecture and governance around them. I led a five-engineer team on a Databricks transportation platform of 300+ production tables. Its metadata-driven Medallion framework meant new source systems arrived as configuration, not as new pipeline code.

I also build AI agents for data engineering workflows - including a voice agent that runs first-round SQL interviews.

My career started in electrical and power-plant engineering. That background still shapes how I think about data: a wrong reading has a real cost.

Architect - Data & AI, Ontology & Semantic Layer

For the logistics domain of a Fortune 500 CPG company, I model shipments, loads, carriers and routes as object types with explicit relationships between them - then define business KPIs as governed measures over that model, rather than as concepts sitting alongside it. The semantic layer maps the model onto the physical transportation tables, so one business question resolves to one governed definition.

No definition ships until it has been recomputed from source and tied out against the figure the business already trusts. That reconciliation gate is the part I would defend hardest - it is the difference between a model agreed in a workshop and one that matches the data underneath it.
